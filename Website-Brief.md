# Website Brief: Foldwell

## Purpose (in order of importance)
1. **Recruit parents for the paper test** and collect a parent waitlist.
2. Explain the idea clearly and build trust, especially about privacy.
3. Be ready to grow into the app's landing page.

Keep it simple. One main page plus a few small pages.

## Audience
Parents and caregivers (18+) of children ages 4 to teen. The site is for parents only. Nothing on it should invite a child to share information.

## Name and domain
The name is **Foldwell** (chosen October 2026 after a USPTO search and domain check). Keep the name in one shared place in the site's code so it is easy to update. Domain: **foldwellnotes.com** (owned). Social: **@foldwellnotes** on Instagram and TikTok.

## Pages
1. **Home**
   - Headline idea: "Notes from home, for life." (placeholder)
   - One-sentence promise: a way for families to trade real words (notes, voice, stickers, drawings) and keep them as a printed keepsake.
   - How it works in 3 steps: Write or record. Share or print. Keep it in a keepsake.
   - Two experiences: the Note Box (ages 4–8) and the Notebook (ages 9+). Use the mockups.
   - Privacy promise strip.
   - Join the test or waitlist button.
2. **For families** (two short sections: little kids; tweens and teens). Explain that the young person is always in control of what they share.
3. **Our privacy promise** (very important)
   - Parent is the account holder.
   - Children don't create accounts or type.
   - No ads, no data selling, no tracking.
   - On-device storage in the first version.
   - Printed keepsakes only include what a tween or teen agrees to.
   - Plain-language summary plus link to the full privacy policy.
4. **Join the test / waitlist**
   - Parent's first name (optional), email, ages of children (bands only: 4–8, 9–12, 13+), checkbox "I am 18 or older and agree to be contacted."
   - Clear message: what we'll send and how to unsubscribe.
   - Do not ask for children's names, photos, or any child information.
5. **About CircleRootTech** (short founder note and mission: purposeful, ad-free apps).
6. **Privacy policy and terms** (needed before the sign-up form goes live; have an attorney review).

## Tone
Warm, honest, simple. Short sentences. Speak to a busy parent. Be clear that this is early and being tested.

## Claims to avoid
- "First," "only," "best," or "no one else does this."
- Any therapy, mental health, or medical claim. This is a family connection tool, not treatment.
- Promising features that are not built. Say "coming soon" or "we're testing."

## What it needs
- Mobile-first, fast, accessible (readable contrast, alt text on images).
- A sign-up form that stores emails with a reputable service. Choose one with clear privacy terms, and explain in the privacy policy where emails go.
- No tracking pixels or third-party analytics until we decide deliberately. If analytics are used later, choose a privacy-friendly option and disclose it.
- Cookie banner only if required by what's added.
